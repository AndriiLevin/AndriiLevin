### What I'm working on

At AkriviaHealth I focus on the intersection of backend services and data infrastructure for a healthcare data platform. Day-to-day this means designing async event-driven services on top of Kafka and AWS event triggers, running PostgreSQL workloads on Kubernetes via the CloudNativePG operator, building Helm charts and Argo CD pipelines for multi-environment GitOps deployments, and prototyping lakehouse workflows on Trino, Iceberg, and AWS Glue. I also maintain a Rust-based ETL platform and provide informal mentorship to junior developers.

Before AkriviaHealth I spent three years at CloverCollab building production FastAPI and Django/DRF backends, optimising PostgreSQL under high-traffic workloads, and standing up Jenkins-based CI/CD pipelines.
